How Did We Start?
We are big fan of 2D Side Scroller RPG games. We played a lot of them but because of low population and insufficient support, they closed one by one. You already know that RPG category is filled with %99 similar games that has no interesting content. Thats why users delete right after first try. So Mehmet and I started a long journey to build the mechanics of our dreams with a custom engine on July 2023 and it turned into a serious game. Hope you like it.
In general we inspired from old games like Golden Axe, Moon Stone, Blaz Blue, Dungeon & Fighter. About mechanics: Dragon Nest, Otherworld Legends, KOF All Star and HiT. About the time and characters: Various mythologies and Mehmet CANPOLAT's noval named Norad. We created a horror / medieval / caotic atmosphere with tension filled moments by blood & sound effects.
And of course, after seeing success stories like this, we thought we had taken the right step. You can see the revenue of Dungeon Fighter Online
This is our first try on the week we start after digging some simple mechanics

Content
Control The Boss
Boss characters are also controllable in game. Gather fragments and be able to summon & control them in some contents.
Brutal Fatalities
Several fatality options depends on your distance to mob, class of mob, health status and last animation status like if mob is in air or on ground.
Smart Enemies
There are 4 difficulties to increase enemy acting & decision speed, also enemies can calculate the range and use skill that can be applied from that range.
PVP & Coop
Real-time & AI pvp is one of content in game. you can play with your friends as rival in Pvp Arena or as team member on each stories and dungeons.
Never Heard Stories
We inspire our stories from Dede Korkut stories. The characters are personally inspired from the people, heroes or demons mentioned in these stories.
